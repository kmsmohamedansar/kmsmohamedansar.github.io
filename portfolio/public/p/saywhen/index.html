How it works

No hotkey. Just look and talk.

The camera and the microphone have to agree before saywhen listens. A camera alone cannot tell chewing from talking. A microphone alone cannot tell who you are talking to.

Five states, zero hotkey

Waiting -> Ready -> Listening -> Writing -> Check

Gaze + lip motion arm the gate. Voice starts recording with a 500 ms audio pre-roll. An adaptive silence window finishes the turn and opens the cancelable preview. No camera? Option + Space (Mac) or Alt + Space (Windows) works any time.

You stay in control

Four ways to send. All cancelable.

Mode 01 · Just pause

Counts down 1.8s, then presses Enter in allowlisted terminals.

If you keep talking, shake your head, press Esc, or switch to another window mid-countdown, nothing is sent. In browsers, email, or chat apps, saywhen types without pressing Enter.

Cancel any time with Esc, a head shake, or by talking again. Or say "scratch that".

Private by design

It sees a few numbers. Never a picture.

Everything runs on your computer. After the speech model downloads, saywhen works with the internet off.

Illustrations drawn from the app's interface, not recordings.

The camera

Each frame is read for a moment, then thrown away. Nothing is saved, logged or uploaded.

Your voice

Speech becomes text on this computer with Whisper, then the audio is discarded. Cloud clean-up is off unless you turn it on.

The Enter key

Pressed only in apps you trust. Commands like rm -rf are typed with a warning and left for you. That check is a heuristic, not a guarantee.

About the install warning. This beta is not signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer asks one extra question. It is about the missing signature. You can also build saywhen yourself from the source.

What platforms are supported today?

Pre-built beta installers cover Apple Silicon Macs (aarch64.dmg) and 64-bit Windows (x64-setup.exe). Automated tests run on both macOS and Windows; hands-on Windows hardware and Intel Mac checks are still open, though Intel Macs can build directly from source.

Does anything leave my computer?

Camera frames are read in memory for face, yaw, and lip movement, then dropped immediately. Speech recognition (Whisper Tiny.en, Base.en, Small.en, and Large v3 Turbo) and voice activity detection (Silero VAD + optional 3-phrase voice enrollment) run on-device. After downloading a speech model, saywhen works completely offline.

What if I don't want it pressing Enter for me?

Pick Type only on Home. It types the cleaned transcript and never sends Enter. Even in default pause mode, Enter is restricted to allowlisted terminals/editors, destructive commands are withheld, and every send shows a cancelable preview.

Can I use it without a webcam or with an external monitor?

Yes. Press ⌥ Space (Mac) or Alt Space (Windows) for manual listening any time, or enable the opt-in wake word (eevee by default) and Side-camera mode for off-center laptop webcams.
